Weekly Protocol Check

Before Buying More

  • Do not buy simply because price is rising.
  • Check whether MVRV Z-Score is still in a reasonable range.
  • Ask: is this accumulation, mid-cycle, or overheating?

Before Selling

  • Do not sell simply because price is falling.
  • Check whether LTH SOPR suggests capitulation or distribution.
  • Ask: is the market exhausting sellers or entering euphoria?

Before Calling a Top

  • Do not call a top based on feeling alone.
  • Check NUPL for historical euphoria or bearish divergence.
  • Ask: has NUPL reached the danger zone?

Before Doing Nothing

  • HODLing is strongest when it is intentional.
  • Check RHODL to see whether conviction capital dominates.
  • Ask: does my written protocol say action is required?
